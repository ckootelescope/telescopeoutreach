Platform framework
Four stages. One continuous loop
Prevent
Stop risk before it moves. Inline interception classifies every outbound communication, document, and workflow trigger against your policy library in real time.
Review
Your team decides. Flagged items queue for human review by your compliance team — not Confinaid staff. Structured, context-aware, defensible.
Improve
Policies get smarter. Analytics surface risk patterns. Policy recommendations adapt to your organization's communication and operational profile.
Prove
Auditable by design. Every action, every decision, every escalation is timestamped, cryptographically logged, and stored as defensible evidence.
