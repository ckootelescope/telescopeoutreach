What It Is
One platform. Every service your pharmacy needs to deliver clinical care.
Independent pharmacies are the most consistent touchpoint in their patients' lives. The problem has never been capability, it's been infrastructure. Outdated tools, fragmented billing, and no physician oversight have kept pharmacists from practicing at the top of their license.
Truentity changes that. Our AI-powered platform combines intelligent patient prioritization, automated care workflows, claims and payer coordination, and physician governance through IndyCare Medical, an affiliated medical group, so your pharmacy can deliver RPM, CCM, and TCM from day one. How It Works
From onboarding to first patient in four steps.
01
Patients are identified automatically
Truentity's AI reviews your patient panel and surfaces the highest-risk patients who qualify for chronic care services with no manual chart reviews needed.
02
Care is delivered by your pharmacist
Your pharmacist connects with patients through structured care calls guided by Truentity's clinical workflows and supported by real-time monitoring data.
03
Physicians provide oversight
IndyCare Medical, an affiliated medical group, provides the clinical governance Medicare requires so your pharmacy stays fully compliant without hiring additional providers.
04
Claims are handled for you
Truentity submits claims and manages payer coordination, so your team documents care instead of chasing paperwork.
Care Management Program
Three care programs. One platform.
RPM
Remote Patient Monitoring
Continuous blood pressure, glucose, and vitals monitoring with real-time alerts and automated monthly reports sent directly to the patient's primary care provider.
CCM
Chronic Care Management
Structured monthly care calls for patients with two or more chronic conditions coordinating medications, tracking goals, and connecting patients to the right resources.
TCM
Transitional Care Management
Post-discharge follow-up that catches medication errors, prevents readmissions, and ensures patients land safely back in their community after a hospital stay.
