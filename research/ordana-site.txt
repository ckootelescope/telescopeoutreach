THE PROBLEM
Your order desk is a bottleneck you can't hire your way out of

Every order that comes in needs a rep to read it, validate it, chase down exceptions, negotiate substitutions, confirm with the retailer, and key it into the ERP. That's a lot of work per order — and it scales linearly with volume.

⏱️
Every order needs a human to close it
Reading, validating, resolving exceptions, confirming back — each step requires a rep's attention before the order can move forward.
🔄
Exceptions stall everything
An out-of-stock item or pricing issue triggers a back-and-forth loop. Orders sit open while reps wait for retailer replies.
📉
More volume means more headcount
The order desk scales linearly with orders. There's no leverage — every new order is more manual work for someone.
