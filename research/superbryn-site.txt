SuperBryn: voice AI reliability platform to evaluate and monitor voice agents, find why they fail and how to fix them.
- Pre-deployment evals: generates test scenarios from the customer's own data sources before launch.
- Production observability: evaluates every production call (not a sample); tracks latency, accuracy, sentiment, task completion across STT, LLM and TTS; sub-second alerting.
- Root cause analysis: traces failures through the voice pipeline to identify whether transcription, reasoning or synthesis broke.
- Compliance: targets regulated industries (healthcare, financial services, insurance) with audit trails and compliance reporting.
- Coming soon: self-learning loop (Observer Agent feeds Improver Agent, which updates the main agent); multi-model routing across providers.
- Integrations (FAQ): Vapi, Retell, Bland.ai, custom implementations.
