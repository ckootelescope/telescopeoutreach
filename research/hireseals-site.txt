How is this different from a chatbot?
+
Chatbots follow scripts. AI Employees have memory, context, and judgment. Valentina remembers a customer's past orders, knows your catalog and per-account negotiated prices, and decides when to escalate to a human. She also writes like a person — not a flowchart.
Can they really capture orders in my ERP?
+
Yes. They quote, capture orders directly in your ERP (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Odoo), generate payment links, and send invoices — over WhatsApp, email, SMS, or phone. You see every step in the dashboard.
Does it work with my current ERP?
+
Yes. Native integration with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Odoo, and QuickBooks. If you use something else, we connect it via API in an onboarding session.
What happens when they don't know the answer?
+
They hand off to a human on your team with full context: conversation history, customer profile, and a suggested response. No endless "let me transfer you" loops.
What about data privacy?
+
Your data is yours. We never use it to train third-party models, never share it, and you can export or delete it at any time.
