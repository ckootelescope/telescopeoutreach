FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we hear from procurement and supply chain leaders.

How much does it cost for suppliers?

Nothing! It's completely free for suppliers.

How are suppliers expected to interact with Axya?

Suppliers don’t need to change their workflow: they can receive a standardized email from Axya users, access a landing page, or connect through a login-less portal on the platform.

What level of involvement is required from Purchasing, IT, and Engineering?

Axya is designed to be deployed with minimal disruption to existing processes and systems.

Purchasing typically leads the project by helping map current workflows, identifying key suppliers, validating business objectives, and driving adoption within the procurement team.

IT is usually involved during the integration phase to support ERP connectivity, security reviews, and data synchronization. Because Axya complements existing ERP systems rather than replacing them, the IT effort is generally focused and time-bound.

Engineering, Operations, or Planning teams may participate to validate visibility requirements, exception management workflows, and reporting needs, but they are rarely heavily involved in the implementation itself.

Most successful projects are led by Procurement with support from IT and key operational stakeholders.

What does a typical 30/60/90-day implementation plan look like?

While many customers are fully deployed within 4 to 6 weeks, a typical 90-day rollout follows three phases:

Days 0-30: Foundation & Integration

- Kickoff and process review
- ERP integration setup
- Initial user training
- Pilot supplier onboarding
- Configuration and testing

Days 31-60: Adoption & Expansion

- Broader supplier onboarding
- Activation of automated workflows
- ERP synchronization validation
- User adoption coaching
- KPI baseline establishment

Days 61-90: Optimization & Scale

- Full deployment across targeted suppliers
- Process optimization
- Visibility and performance reporting
- ROI measurement
- Expansion to additional sites, teams, or business units if applicable

Our team works closely with customers throughout the process to ensure a smooth transition and rapid time to value.

How do customers ensure successful user adoption?

The most successful deployments focus on outcomes rather than software.

Internally, adoption is driven by helping buyers spend less time on supplier follow-ups, ERP maintenance, and status requests, while giving them more time for strategic activities such as supplier development, sourcing, and risk management.

For suppliers, adoption is accelerated because Axya adapts to their preferred way of working. Suppliers can collaborate through email, portal access, landing pages, EDI, APIs, or other communication methods, minimizing the need for significant behavioral change.

Customers typically position Axya as a procurement transformation initiative that improves visibility, accountability, and efficiency rather than as a technology project.

What are the most common implementation challenges?

Like any transformation initiative, there are a few common challenges:

Supplier onboarding

Engaging suppliers early and communicating the benefits clearly is important to accelerate participation and maximize value.

ERP data quality

Many organizations discover that purchase order information, supplier records, or existing processes need refinement before automation can deliver its full impact.

Change management

Moving from reactive procurement processes to more proactive and strategic workflows often requires adjustments to established habits and responsibilities.

Integration planning

While ERP integrations are typically straightforward, alignment on business rules, data ownership, and workflow expectations helps ensure a successful deployment.

Our implementation team has experience guiding manufacturers through these challenges and follows a proven onboarding process to help customers achieve value quickly.

Who is Axya for?

Axya is built for manufacturers that rely on a large supplier network and would like to scale their procurement operations more efficiently.

Our customers typically:

- Manage hundreds of suppliers and purchase orders
- Operate complex supply chains across one or multiple sites
- Use ERP systems such as SAP, Oracle, Infor, Epicor, or NetSuite
- Have procurement teams spending too much time on operational work
- Need better supplier visibility and more accurate ERP data
- Are growing and want to scale without continuously adding headcount

Axya is particularly well suited for organizations in:

- Aerospace & Defense Manufacturing
- Transportation & Automotive
- Mining & Resources
- Industrial Equipment & Machinery
- Space & Advanced Manufacturing
- Packaging & Paper Products
- Custom Manufacturing & Metal Fabrication

Many of our customers come to us because they recognize one or more of the following challenges:

- We're constantly firefighting
- We spend too much time chasing suppliers
- We discover delays too late
- We're growing faster than our team can scale
- Our ERP doesn't always reflect supplier reality

If these challenges sound familiar, Axya can help connect your suppliers, buyers, and ERP systems to create real-time visibility, reduce manual work, and enable procurement teams to focus on more strategic initiatives.

Does Axya integrate with my ERP?

Yes! Axya is made to complete your ERPs. Axya doesn't replace an ERP, it augments it.
