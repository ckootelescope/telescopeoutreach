Process
01 Research
We start by understanding what you're building, how the model will be used, and where data quality matters most. From there, we define target use cases, speakers, environments, and edge cases, and translate them into clear dataset requirements and acceptance criteria.
02 Collect
Next, we execute the plan with end-to-end collection and production handled in-house. We collect audio to match your spec, then run rigorous QA, transcription, and review to ensure consistency and reliability. We share checkpoints and samples along the way so you can validate direction early and iterate quickly before scaling.
03 Iterate
Once the core dataset is delivering value, we help you grow coverage and performance over time. We identify gaps from model results and real-world feedback, then expand into new accents, noise conditions, domains, and long-tail scenarios. Each dataset evolves alongside your product and keeps getting more useful with every release.
