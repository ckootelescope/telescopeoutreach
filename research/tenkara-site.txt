Built By Those That Have Made Products In Factories

Before I built Tenkara, I launched two manufacturing plants in Cape Canaveral, Florida.

What I remember most is the coordination. Chasing suppliers who wouldn't call back. Freight moving without anyone knowing where. Prices that changed before the PO cleared. It was constant, it was invisible, and it ate the people I could least afford to lose.

ERP records what happened. We still had to manage all the grunt work first. Every field in that database got there because someone opened an inbox, made a call, chased a number, and typed it in.

Tenkara takes that work.

Command centers that run the office side of your business so your hours move to the floor, where product gets made.

This is your operational advantage.

Ben Stern,
CEO of Tenkara
