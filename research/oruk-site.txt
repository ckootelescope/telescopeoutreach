Common questions
What exactly does Oruk do?
Oruk turns speech into transcripts, speaker turns, and labels for emotion and delivery. Choose a model for recorded audio or live conversations.
How is it different from speech-to-text?
Speech-to-text tells you what was said. Oruk also analyzes how it sounded: frustrated, excited, hesitant, sarcastic, and more.
What can I build with it?
Voice agents with more context, searchable call reviews, expressive captions, research tools, and other products that work with speech.
