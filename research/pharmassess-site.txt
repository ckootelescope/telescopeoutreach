Medical Directives
Extend your pharmacist's prescribing authority beyond standard scope through a Collaborative Prescribing Agreement (CPA) with a partner physician. Medical directives allow pharmacists to independently initiate, modify, or discontinue drug therapy under pre-approved protocols - without requiring a patient-specific order each time.
Structured CPA-based workflows
PharmAssess guides pharmacists through directive-specific assessment pathways aligned to your CPA's standing orders, ensuring every intervention is clinically appropriate, consistently documented, and audit-ready.
Expanded patient care capacity
Deliver physician-level interventions for chronic disease management, acute care, and preventive health - improving patient outcomes while generating new billable service opportunities for your pharmacy.
