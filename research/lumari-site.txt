Frequently Asked Questions
What is AI-powered procurement automation?

Lumari is the first AI platform purpose-built for direct materials procurement teams. Lumari automates the tactical work slowing down supply chain teams. AI agents run sourcing and RFQs end-to-end, follow up with suppliers, extract and normalize quotes from emails and PDFs, track purchase orders, and escalate risks early. Instead of burying your team in email and spreadsheets, Lumari handles the repetitive coordination so they can focus on strategic decisions.

How does Lumari handle sourcing and procurement?

Lumari automates sourcing and procurement end-to-end. On the sourcing side, AI agents send RFQs, track supplier responses, extract pricing and lead times from any format, and normalize everything for apples-to-apples comparison. On the procurement side, agents keep purchase orders moving by following up with suppliers, capturing status updates, and flagging risks before they become problems.

How does Lumari integrate with existing procurement and ERP systems?

Lumari integrates with existing ERP and procurement systems and layers on top of your current stack with no migration or rip-and-replace required. AI agents pull data from your system of record, take action, and push updates back automatically. Your ERP stays the source of truth, just more accurate and up to date.

What types of companies use Lumari?

Lumari is built for companies that build or buy physical products, from growing startups scaling their supply chains to Fortune 500 enterprises managing hundreds of suppliers. That includes manufacturers, hardware companies, consumer goods brands, and industrial businesses across electronics, energy systems, modular building, and infrastructure equipment. If your procurement team is buried in supplier email and spreadsheets, Lumari is built for you.

How is Lumari different from traditional procurement software?

Traditional procurement tools were built for indirect spend like office supplies and SaaS licenses, where purchases are simple and predictable. Direct materials procurement is fundamentally harder: suppliers send quotes in different formats, lead times shift constantly, and a single missed update can have many downstream effects. Lumari is purpose-built for this complexity. AI agents send RFQs, extract quotes from unstructured emails and PDFs, follow up with suppliers, and surface real-time PO status so your team stays ahead of problems instead of chasing them.

How long does it take to get started with Lumari?

Most teams go live with Lumari within weeks. There is no heavyweight implementation project. Lumari adapts to your existing workflows, so your team does not need to change how they operate. Agents learn your processes, terminology, and supplier relationships, and continously improve.
