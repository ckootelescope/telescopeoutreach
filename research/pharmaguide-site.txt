About PharmaGuide
PharmaGuide was built by pharmacists who were doing the paperwork themselves. It is Canadian, hosted in Canada, and has been in pharmacies since 2018 — from single independents to a national chain running it across hundreds of locations.
Built for how pharmacy actually works
Community & retail pharmacies
Patients book online, pharmacists document the assessment, and the claim goes out — without re-keying anything into a second system. Fewer clicks per service, more services per day.
Chains & enterprise
Standardise clinical programs across every location, with the reporting head office needs and the guardrails compliance needs. Deployed at national scale in Canada.
Manufacturers & industry partners
Reach patients where care is delivered, and see consented, structured real-world evidence from 1,000+ community pharmacies.
Institutions & care networks
Run funded programs across regions with role-based oversight, auditable records and measurable outcomes.
Modular. Flexible. Built for pharmacies.
