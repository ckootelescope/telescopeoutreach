Building the intelligence layer for the physical technology supply chain

Your enterprise stack stores the data.
It never makes the decision.

PLM owns the design, ERP owns the transaction, MES owns the line, and distributors own availability. None of them own the continuously changing question of what an OEM should actually do next. ChipHub is built for that decision.

Fragmented across systems

Component, supplier, price, lead-time, and risk data live in disconnected tools. The judgment that ties them together lives in spreadsheets and inboxes.

Decisions made after the fact

Most hardware economics are set at design time, yet sourcing starts only after engineering has already locked the component in.

No closed loop

When a part goes at-risk, the change back through sourcing, purchasing, and the BOM is manual, slow, and rarely optimal.

One decision layer, from schematic to sustaining

Component selection, supplier selection, sourcing, and risk are not separate problems. ChipHub reasons across the full lifecycle so every decision reflects the same cost, quality, lead-time, and supply-risk constraints.
