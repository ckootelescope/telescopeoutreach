WHAT LITEWAVE DOES

Accelerate quality and manufacturing workflows

Litewave reviews the records your site produces using your specifications, procedures and applicable regulations, then gives your team the exceptions with the supporting evidence attached. Batch release is where most sites start. The same engine supports investigations, incoming materials, QMS documentation and quality trending. It works with your existing MES, LIMS and eBPR data, as well as handwritten records that most systems cannot read.

Batch Review & Release

Litewave applies your master record, your specifications and your release criteria to every operation in every batch, then gives QA a review-by-exception file with the evidence attached. Your reviewers keep the decision and get their time back. It works with your eBPR and MES data, as well as handwritten records that most systems cannot read.

Deviation Investigations

Litewave builds the timeline from the source records, runs the recurrence assessment across your full deviation and CAPA history, and proposes root cause and CAPA candidates with the evidence for and against each. HOW WE DEPLOY

Bring your manufacturing memory to life

Years of executed records, investigations and supplier history already hold the answer to most quality questions at your site. Deployment is how they start answering. Litewave reads what your site already produces, proves itself next to your reviewers, and goes live under the standard your site is held to.

1
Connect what you already run

Litewave reads your executed records where they sit, on paper, in scans, and in your MES, LIMS and eBPR. There is no migration project and no SOP rewrite. Our reference deployment runs on 100 to 150 page paper batch records with no eBPR migration at all.

2
Run alongside your reviewers

Litewave reviews the same batches your team is reviewing manually, and you compare findings for an agreed period. Nothing changes in your process until that comparison satisfies your quality unit.

3

You get the validation documentation needed to qualify Litewave in your environment. Cloud sites typically see results within 5 days of onboarding. On-premises, the system comes online in under 8 hours once the hardware is on site, powered and on the network.

Extend to the next workflow

The same agents run identically across cloud, on-premises and air-gapped installs. Adding a workflow, a line or another site is configuration rather than a second implementation programme.

TRENDING, DRIFT DETECTION AND YIELD INTELLIGENCE
Litewave Optimize

Litewave Optimize turns the structured records behind Digitize and Comply into live quality and process data, so drift, deviation trends and the variables behind yield variation are visible while the year is still running rather than reconstructed at review time. It works on what your site has executed, which is what makes it defensible.

Live Trending and Drift Detection

Statistical trending against your own alert and action limits by product, site, line, equipment and shift, with drift and rising frequency surfaced while a result is still inside specification rather than after it breaches. Cycle-time anomalies and incoming material quality issues are tracked as signals in their own right, because both move yield before anything shows up in a release result.

Yield Performance Drivers

Yield varying inside acceptable ranges with no way to explain it is the exact problem our API customer brought us, and the cause was that batch, analytical and equipment data were never joined. Litewave joins them, so the variables that move with yield are identifiable and can be tested rather than argued about.

Deviation Prediction

A domain-trained model reads your own deviation and batch history and flags where current conditions resemble those that preceded past events, with the prior deviations and their outcomes cited so an investigator can judge the signal instead of taking a score on trust.

What-If Analysis

Run scenarios across sites, lines and recipes using the batches you have actually executed, so the operating envelope worth investigating is narrowed on the record rather than on the line. It reasons from what your process has done, not from a mechanistic model of what it should do, which is the honest limit of the method and also why it needs no instrumentation project to stand it up.

Golden Batch Comparison

Compare a running batch against your best executed batches for the same product, within a site and across every site that runs it, on the parameters your records actually captured. What a good batch looked like becomes specific and retrievable rather than remembered, and a site inheriting a product inherits the settings behind it instead of rediscovering them.

Site and Supplier Benchmarking

Where the same product runs at more than one site, or the same material arrives from more than one supplier, performance is compared on one data model instead of four spreadsheets that disagree.
