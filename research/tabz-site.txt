Frequently asked questions
What is Tabz?
Tabz is the Unified Pharmacy Commerce Platform. It connects checkout, payment collection, patient communication, and fulfillment into one operating layer that sits natively inside pharmacy workflows. Pharmacies use Tabz to collect payment before dispensing, automate patient outreach, reduce return-to-stock, and grow OTC revenue, all through a single platform that integrates directly with their pharmacy management system.
Which types of pharmacies does Tabz work with?
Tabz works across the full pharmacy spectrum: retail, compounding, specialty, mail-order, and hub pharmacies. It also supports pharma manufacturers building direct-to-patient programs, pharmacy management system vendors adding a commerce layer to their platform, and technology companies embedding pharmacy payment capabilities into their products. Each pharmacy type has workflows specific to how they operate, and Tabz is built to fit all of them.
Which pharmacy management systems does Tabz integrate with?
Tabz integrates natively with PioneerRx, Liberty Software, Keycentrix, PK Software, Computer-Rx, SiCompounding, and EPS. Prescription data, copay amounts, and patient records flow directly from the PMS into the Tabz checkout flow, with no manual re-entry required.
Is Tabz HIPAA-compliant and PCI DSS certified?
Yes. Tabz is HIPAA-ready and Level 1 PCI DSS certified. Protected health information and payment data are handled according to HIPAA and PCI DSS standards, built directly into the infrastructure rather than added on. Pharmacies, technology partners, and pharma manufacturers that build on Tabz inherit this compliance posture without having to obtain or maintain those certifications independently.
