What We Do
We offer a fully cloud-based Canadian pharmacy suite, from point of sale to dispensing. Access faxes, fill prescriptions, and deliver clinical services from any computer, anywhere. No need to rely on servers at your pharmacy.
How We Do It
No more juggling multiple software platforms. Dispensing, point of sale, clinical services, a patient-facing app, and even incident reporting. Everything your pharmacy needs, packaged into one Box.
