How
MEDZOOMER
Works?
Our secure delivery process makes it easy for patients to receive their prescriptions quickly and without unnecessary hassle.
Step 1: Order
The Patient places a prescription delivery order through the provider’s platform.
Step 2: Fill
The Pharmacy prepares the prescription to be sent out to the Patient.
Step 3: Delivery
The prescriptions are picked up for delivery by a HIPAA certified courier.
Step 4: Relax!
The prescriptions are delivered successfully to the patient’s door.
