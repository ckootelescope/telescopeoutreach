How is Arcessio different from traditional sourcing platforms?
Traditional sourcing platforms help you buy the part you've already specified. Arcessio starts with the engineering data, determines the best manufacturing path, then carries those decisions through supplier selection and production execution.
How does Arcessio protect our engineering data?
Protecting customer intellectual property is fundamental to our platform. We use enterprise-grade security controls, role-based access, encryption in transit and at rest, and strict access policies to safeguard your engineering data.
Is our data used to train AI models?
No. Your confidential engineering data, CAD files, drawings, and manufacturing information are not used to train Arcessio's or third-party AI models without your explicit permission.
Can Arcessio analyze hundreds of parts at once?
Yes. Arcessio is designed to analyze individual parts, assemblies, or entire BOMs at scale. Our AI can evaluate hundreds of parts simultaneously to identify manufacturing processes, materials, should-cost estimates, qualified suppliers, and opportunities to reduce cost, lead time, and complexity.
How does Arcessio build its supplier network?
We carefully qualify manufacturing partners based on capabilities, quality systems, certifications, production capacity, and other technical requirements. Our AI then matches each part with the suppliers best suited to manufacture it.
Can I trust AI manufacturing recommendations?
Arcessio is designed to augment engineering expertise—not replace it. Our AI provides transparent recommendations that engineers and procurement teams can review, validate, and refine before production decisions are made.

ABOUT ARCESSIO
We believe manufacturing should move as fast as innovation.
WHY WE EXIST
We founded Arcessio to change that.
Today, even the most advanced product companies move from design to production through fragmented tools, spreadsheets, emails, supplier searches, and manual handoffs. Critical engineering context is lost, requirements are recreated, and sourcing often starts from scratch. As companies add more parts, suppliers, and production volume, these inefficiencies slow launches and make manufacturing harder to scale.
Our AI agents turn engineering data into manufacturing decisions and carry those decisions through procurement and production. We help teams identify the right process, material, production economics, and qualified supplier for every part - making it easier to launch products, scale production on demand, and build agile, localized supply chains.
