§01 / THE GAP - problem: observe != intervene
You evaluated 5%. You shipped 100%.
That's the gap. Sampled scoring, unchecked judges and pre-release test suites tell you how your agents behaved in the lab. None of it tells you what your agents are doing right now, on live traffic, in front of real customers.
5% EVALUATED / 95% LIVE, UNWATCHED
01 The 95% you didn't sample - The run that loses the account is one you never scored. You find out from the customer, not the dashboard.
02 Who judges the judge? - One biased judge poisons every score downstream. You're making ship or kill calls on numbers nobody verified.
03 Static evals are not in prod - The run that breaks in front of a customer was never in your test set. Your suite passes while prod burns.

§02 / CLOSED LOOP - spine: observe -> evaluate -> act
THE RELIABILITY TRIFECTA
Observability stops at the dashboard. Reliability needs a loop.
Other tools observe and score, then hand you the problem. Prefactor wires evaluations and risk straight into action: pause a risky run for approval, or enforce a policy at runtime, through the SDK or API.
Observe every run as traces & spans
Evaluate with the evals you define: LLM-as-judge, technical & qualitative metrics, plus data-risk
Act: hold, approve or block, human-in-the-loop, via SDK or API
A risky agent is caught, not just charted.

Bring any datasource into the agent run.
Custom spans aren't just markers. Pull context from any datasource (GitHub, Linear, Jira, your database, internal APIs) into the run, so every evaluation is grounded in what actually happened.

§05 / LIFECYCLE - promote: dev -> staging -> prod
THE AGENT DEVELOPMENT LIFECYCLE
Ship agents like software: versioned, staged, promoted.
Prefactor versions every agent, tracks it against a schema, and promotes it through dev, staging and prod only when its evals pass, so you can compare scores version-to-version and prove each one is better than the last.
Versioning - Immutable versions by semver, commit or tag, with lifecycle states from pending to retired, so you always know exactly which agent ran.
Schema management - Two-tier schema-and-deployment versioning, tracked independently, with conformance drift surfaced when a run breaks its declared shape.
Environments - First-class dev / staging / prod. Every deployment binds a version to an environment, with eval-gated promotion and instant rollback.
