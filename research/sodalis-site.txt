questions
Questions, answered.
Do you integrate with my systems?
Yes. We connect to the systems your pharmacy already runs on, including the major pharmacy management systems. If we haven’t integrated with yours yet, we scope it into the implementation.
How long does implementation take?
We typically deploy in about four weeks and reach target performance within three months. IT involvement is minimal: we manage the rollout and work most directly with your pharmacy and operations teams.
How do you price?
Pricing is usage-based and tiered, with a base platform fee plus a one-time implementation fee. It scales with your volume, so what you pay tracks the value you get.
How accurate is it, and what happens when Axon isn’t sure?
Axon is built to know its limits. When something is ambiguous or high-risk, it flags the item and routes it to your team instead of guessing. Your licensed pharmacists make the final call, and you can see exactly what Axon did and why.
Do you train your models on our data?
No. Your data stays yours. We use it only to run and improve your own Axon configuration, never to train models for other customers.
What does this mean for my team?
Axon takes the repetitive, manual work off your team’s plate so their time goes to patients and the decisions that need real clinical judgment. It lets you raise your standard of care and handle far more volume without scaling headcount.
