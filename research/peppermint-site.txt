Peppermint is built to remove work, not add it. We handle routine tasks and investigations independently, and involve your team only when an approval, a correction, or clinical context is genuinely required.
Peppermint works across the systems and data sources you already use — CTMS, EDC or eSource, contracts, sponsor portals, email, bank data, and accounting systems. We connect to your existing environment rather than forcing a rip-and-replace implementation.
Peppermint determines what is owed, prepares invoices, tracks outstanding revenue, follows up with sponsors and CROs, reconciles payments, investigates exceptions, and provides financial reporting. Your team keeps the decisions that need clinical context or institutional authority: approving exceptions, confirming what happened at a visit, and any judgment call about the study itself.
Peppermint checks financial activity against contracts, amendments, and available source data rather than assuming every system is correct. Missing or conflicting information is flagged for investigation.
AI structures contracts, interprets fragmented data, and prepares the work — but it doesn't operate without controls. Our billers review consequential outputs and handle anything ambiguous, contradictory, or requiring judgment.
Follow-ups are branded and written to sound like your site, and our billers handle sensitive conversations directly and professionally. What sponsors and CROs experience is a site that bills accurately and follows up consistently.
Our revenue diagnostic reviews historical activity, contracts, amendments, invoices, and payments to identify missed billables, unpaid items, incorrect rates, and unreconciled cash. Many customers start here, then expand into ongoing revenue operations.
