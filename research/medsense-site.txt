How it works
A turnkey path from enrollment to revenue.
Medsense delivers the technology, workflows, and clinical operations to stand up a revenue-generating monitoring or adherence program in weeks — not quarters.
01 / Identify
Patient identification & program matching
We review your panel, match patients to the program that fits, and route them into onboarding.
02 / Enroll
Onboarding & consent
Outreach, scheduling, and informed consent handled by our clinical operations team — with full audit trail.
03 / Operate
Monitoring & intervention
Connected devices stream adherence and physiologic data. Triggered events become pharmacist or care-team interventions.
04 / Earn
Pharmacy revenue
Medsense handles documentation, billing, and payer claims under our clinical entity. Pharmacies earn a flat $25 per enrolled patient, per month, per program for the monthly care management review call and documentation — no billing complexity, no reimbursement risk.
