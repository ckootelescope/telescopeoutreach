MATURITY JOURNEY
Four stages,
built with you.
Every manufacturer is at a different stage, but the path is the same: digitize the floor, automate the inspection, turn it into insight, then close the loop with agentic action. We build each stage with you, not around you.
STAGE 01
Digitization
Capture what's happening on your floor.
Loopr Inspect digitizes inspections and standardizes workflows across operators, shifts, and sites while creating structured data for what comes next.
STAGE 02
Automation
Put AI vision to work.
Loopr AI Inspect automates repetitive quality checks where speed, consistency, and precision matter, while keeping inspectors in control.
STAGE 03
Visualization/Insights
Understand what is driving quality issues.
Loopr analyzes inspection data to uncover defect trends, recurring problems, and probable root causes, with the evidence to support its findings.
STAGE 04
Agentic Workflows
Coming soon: turn insights into action.
Loopr is building AI agents that will connect quality insights to your existing systems and recommend actions such as flagging a ticket, quarantining a lot, or pausing a line for your team to review and approve.

FROM PILOT TO FULL ROLLOUT
Four Weeks To
A Working Solution
The pilot isn't a proof-of-concept for its own sake: it's how we establish the platform and the model on your floor, fast, so Stage 1 (Digitization) has real trust and value to build on.
WEEK 0
Discovery & process
Understand the process, inspection requirements, systems, and deployment environment. Define the pilot scope, success criteria, and data collection plan.
WEEK 1
Data Collection & Forward Deployed Engineering
Start collecting data on the factory floor using tablets or camera systems. A forward deployed engineer works alongside your team to understand the process, capture real production scenarios, and identify edge cases.
WEEK 2
Data Labelling
& Preprocessing
Prepare the collected data for model training. Label images, organize datasets, and apply the required preprocessing to create a high quality training set.
WEEK 3
Model Training
Train the AI model using the prepared data. Evaluate its performance against the defined requirements and continue training as needed to improve accuracy.
WEEK 4 →
Deployment, Validation
& Refinement
Deploy the model in the production environment and validate it against real inspections. Refine the model using feedback and edge cases from the factory floor, then confirm readiness for scale.
