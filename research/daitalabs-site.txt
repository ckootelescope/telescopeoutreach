Why does a garment order still travel as WhatsApp screenshots, email chains and spreadsheets?

Daita Labs started in 2024 with a question that wouldn't go away. Luis and Jérôme could have guessed at the answer from a desk. Instead they went and looked.

They spent a year wherever clothes are actually designed, bought and made, from London and Spain to Italy, Germany, Portugal, Vietnam, Bangladesh and Delhi. Then they did the thing most software founders don't: they moved to Tiruppur, India's knitwear capital, and built Daita Labs inside a working factory, next to their first customer, until it fit the way merchandisers, factories, buying offices and brands really work.

Along the way they raised $1.2M. Today Daita Labs is run from Bangalore, and the phone still rings from Tiruppur every day.

FAQ

Questions we get asked first.

If yours is not here, feel free to reach out to us.

Do our factories have to change how they report?

No. Daita Labs reads the WhatsApp updates, cut plans and reports your factories already send. Line supervisors keep working the way they do today; Daita Labs does the structuring.

Which languages does the agent handle?

Five at launch: English, Tamil, Hindi, Bengali and Urdu, with Vietnamese following. Supervisors message in their own language and the timeline is written in yours.

Does Daita Labs replace our ERP?

No. Daita Labs sits between the messy inputs (chat, email, spreadsheets) and your systems of record. It gives everyone one live production timeline and can push clean, confirmed data into your ERP so nothing is retyped.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live within two weeks. We connect your existing groups and PO formats, onboard your merchandisers, and run the first orders alongside you.

Who can see what?

You decide. Brands see their own POs across all suppliers; buying offices see the brands and factories they manage; factories see their own lines. Every update is timestamped and attributed, so accountability is built in.

How is Daita Labs priced?

A simple monthly subscription priced per tracking item and per seat, with onboarding included. Talk to us for a quote based on your own volumes and team size.
