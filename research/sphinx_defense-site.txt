Sphinx Contact Optimization & Planning Engine (SCOPe)
SCOPe helps eliminate one of the biggest bottlenecks in satellite operations: coordinating limited ground resources for data transfer. By identifying optimal time windows to downlink and uplink data, SCOPE, a Sphinx proprietary platform, ensures timely, efficient contact between satellites and ground stations.
