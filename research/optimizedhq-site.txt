Is our data safe?
Yes. SOC 2 Type I and II, ISO 27001, ISO 42001 and GDPR. Every customer’s data is isolated and encrypted. Full details in our Trust Center.
How much work is this for our team?
One point of contact and a set of data exports. No integration is needed to start. We work alongside your team, on-site when it matters.
Does this replace our ERP?
No. Optimized works on top of the systems you already run.
What does it cost?
The audit is a fixed price. You see what it finds, sized in dollars, before you commit to anything else.
