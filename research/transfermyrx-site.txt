Our Products
State of the art platform for pharmacies to transfer prescriptions
efficiently,
accurately and securely.
The Problem
Transferring prescriptions to an unaffiliated pharmacy with a different software system
is a
very tedious task that every pharmacy is asked to complete several times a day. It
requires
a Pharmacist from Pharmacy A to get on the
phone with a Pharmacist from Pharmacy B to verify the validity of the prescription. This
process can take anywhere from 5 to 30 or more minutes to complete.
Our Solution
TransferMyRx is a new technology portal that enables pharmacists to transfer
prescriptions
securely and efficiently to any pharmacy in the United States. TransferMyRx helps
pharmacists replace the time-consuming task of manually
transferring a prescription to an unaffiliated pharmacy with an error-free, direct
communication transfer system. Our technology saves time and reduces labor costs, giving
pharmacists more time to engage with their patients.
Accountability for Patients
TransferMyRx was built to accommodate the patient as well. It can be a long and
frustrating
process trying to get your pharmacy to transfer your prescription to an unaffiliated
pharmacy. TransferMyRx allows patients to quickly
request a transfer of their prescription from Pharmacy A to Pharmacy B, safely and
securely.
