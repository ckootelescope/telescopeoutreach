FAQ
What is Bifrost? Bifrost is the fastest open-source LLM gateway, introducing just 11 microseconds of overhead at 5k RPS on a t3.xlarge machine. Built for reliability, security, and scale, it provides a unified layer for managing model access, enforcing guardrails, and ensuring governance across AI systems.
How is my data protected? Bifrost offers zero-touch in-VPC deployments, so no data ever leaves your environment or passes through Bifrost/Maxim servers.
