Product Manager
As we continue to grow in revenue and customers, we are looking to bring on a product manager. As a PM in a product-focused company, you will become the go-to expert of our platform. You will understand our customers inside and out, and you'll develop an intuition around (and frameworks to assess) which investments will best improve the customer experience and our business overall. You'll be working closely with our engineering and UI/UX team. You'll be talking with and observing customers. You'll be running queries in our database and constructing other internal data sources to understand what's working and what needs to be improved.
About you:
You've got 1-3 years of experience as a product manager, management consultant, or data analyst. You have a bachelor's degree with a major in a quantitative field (ideally computer science). You're whip smart, both in terms of connecting dots and emotional intelligence. You enjoy quantitative analysies, and you're highly technical. You're also creative and thoughtful. You're a good listener. You have an insatiable curiosity and enjoy working with other smart people. You don't sweat the small stuff because you love the small stuff and excel at it. You love the big stuff even more. Others have commented on how strong your attention to detail is. You raised an eyebrow at the typo in this job description and now, upon reading this sentence, appreciate that it was intentional.
NYC-based is preferred, but much of our work is remote.
​
​
Compensation: $100k-$150k + equity.

Pharmacy Experience Manager
As a Pharmacy Experience Manager, you'll be the primary contact for our pharmacy accounts. You'll play a key role in onboarding, building, and supporting long-lasting relationships with pharmacies using our platform. This is a high-impact, customer-facing role that is ideal for someone with pharmacy experience who is detail-oriented and solutions-focused.
What You'll Do:
Own and manage relationships, serving as the primary contact for onboarding, issues, and questions
Lead onboarding and training, ensuring pharmacy teams are set up for success
Troubleshoot and resolve issues with thoughtfulness and urgency, prioritizing accordingly
Gather feedback to improve product and customer experience
Collaborate cross-functionally with our product and engineering teams to maximize engagement and satisfaction
Advocate for your accounts, anticipating needs and providing proactive guidance to maximize value on the platform
Drive growth and help expand existing relationships
About you:
​
Pharmacy experience required (ie. pharmacy technician) - you understand the pharmacy workflow
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills - you know how to build trust quickly and understand your accounts' needs
Tech-savvy - comfortable with technology and quick to learn new tools
Exceptionally detail-oriented - you catch the small things. Let me know your favorite color when you apply
Highly organized - you are able to juggle multiple accounts and take on unexpected issues when they arise
Thrive in a startup environment - you are adaptable, collaborative, and self-motivated
Bonus if you have worked in account management or training roles
