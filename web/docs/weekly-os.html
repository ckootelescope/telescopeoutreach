Weekly Operating System

You type the week. It structures what you said, places it around the calls you already have, and keeps it somewhere you can look at. It does not decide what you work on.

01

What it does and does not do

Does

  • Turns a paragraph into discrete to-dos
  • Places them around your real calendar
  • Splits weekly commitments from dated work
  • Expands “the relevant follow-ups” into the actual list, because the outreach database knows
  • Carries what you did not finish into next week
  • Answers a question when you ask one

Does not

  • Invent work you did not mention
  • Decide how to balance your week
  • Volunteer suggestions unprompted
  • Change your Affinity records
  • Send anything

Suggestions are pull, not push. When you ask for investors to connect with, it goes and builds that list. When you do not ask, it stays quiet.

04

The chat, all week

SUN

The paragraph. It writes tasks and shows you the week. Wrong reading? Say so in the box.

MID

“Read the Pathwork doc, competitor list is half done, punt the call notes to next week.” Status changes and reschedules on rows that already exist — never a regenerated week.

ASK

“What is still open on Pathwork?” “Which investors have I not talked to since spring?” “What did I get done last week?”

FRI

The week closes. Unfinished tasks keep their company or market and lose their date, so they are waiting on Sunday rather than gone.

Hard constraint

No model-authored SQL. A fixed read bundle in, and a closed set of writes out: add_task, set_status, reschedule, drop_task, note. The service-role key bypasses row-level security on every founder email in the database.

05

Market deep dives

The component you asked for, in v1 rather than later.

A market is a container the same way a company is: it holds reading, Harmonic sourcing against the theme, expert and operator calls, market outreach, and what you learned. The overlap with sourcing is real and does not need resolving — the stream is just a label, and the subject is the structure. Harmonic sourcing against a theme is stream: sourcing, subject: market. It shows up in both places because it genuinely belongs to both.

Your own paragraph makes the case. Pathwork is life insurance. Bhargav has insurance experts. The September conference is InsureTech. Three separate sentences pointing at one market, and today nothing holds them together, so the third time you go looking for insurance experts you start over.

That is also where the knowledge compounds. The outreach engine already sweeps your Granola calls for market patterns every time it drafts an email, and it re-derives them from scratch each time. If the deep dive is a durable place, the drafting reads what the diligence learned, and the same expert call pays twice.

06

Schema

A task points at a company, a market, or a person. That is what lets one table serve every stream, and what lets the later views land on data already there.

task
  id, title, notes
  stream       diligence | sourcing | investor | market | learning
  subject_kind company | market | person | none
  subject_id
  week_id      → week      null = backlog, survives the week
  day          date           null = weekly item, no day yet
  due_on       date           can sit beyond this week (the conference)
  calendar_ref                the event it is anchored to, if any
  origin       calvin | expanded | asked_for
  ref_kind, ref_id            the step / sequence it came from
  status       open | done | moved | dropped
  created_at, done_at

market                      a deep dive, first class
  id, name, status, opened_at, notes
  links: tasks, Granola calls, companies, experts

person                      investors, operators, experts
  id, name, firm, kind, affinity_id
  market_id                   the deep dive they came from
  last_contact_at

company_list                read from Affinity saved views
  company_id, list      priority | hard_to_crack
  tp_status, tp_owner, next_steps, synced_at

week      id, week_of, status, generated_at, review
message   id, week_id, role, content, actions_taken, created_at

origin is worth a column: calvin is what you said, expanded is a fan-out from something you said, asked_for is a suggestion you requested. Nothing else can write a task.
